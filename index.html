Simple Operations

How panichaiyo Operates

A seamless 3-stage flow connecting Kathmandu customers with neighborhood water delivery partners.

Stage 01

Customer Places Order

Households and offices request 20L jars or sealed bottle packs on the Pani Chaiyo platform.

Stage 02

Local Partner Dispatches

The nearest verified water partner accepts the order, loads sanitized jars onto their delivery vehicle, and routes their driver.

Stage 03

Doorstep Drop & Instant Pay

Rider carries jars to the customer's doorstep with digital QR handover. Partner receives guaranteed weekly settlement.
